Getting Started

What is general outpatient therapy?

Our general outpatient services offer flexible, individual therapy for children, teens, adults, couples, and families. Sessions typically last 45–60 minutes, and you and your clinician decide together how often to meet.

Every plan is guided by your goals and grounded in evidence-based treatment for OCD, anxiety, and related conditions.

Who do we help?

Children, teens, and adults living with OCD, anxiety, or related conditions, including:

  • Intrusive thoughts and worry
  • Panic and phobias
  • Perfectionism and school or work stress
  • Body-focused repetitive behaviors

See the full list on our Who We Serve page.

Our Approach

What is ERP and ACT?

ERP

Exposure and Response Prevention is a form of CBT and the first-line psychological treatment for OCD. It involves gradually facing fears and resisting the compulsions or rituals that follow them.

ACT

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy helps people change their relationship with difficult thoughts and feelings through mindfulness, acceptance, and values-based action.

We often combine ERP and ACT, and may add other evidence-based strategies. Learn more on our Treatment page.

What might therapy include?

  • Gradually approaching situations you've been avoiding
  • Learning tools to manage anxious thoughts
  • Identifying your personal values to guide choices

Your First Steps

How do I get started?

Step 1
Reach out. Fill out our contact form.
Step 2
Consultation call. Talk with our support team about what you're looking for.
Step 3
Get matched. We pair you with a clinician who fits your needs, with a chance to connect before treatment begins.
Step 4
Begin treatment. A diagnostic assessment shapes a treatment plan tailored to you.

Need more support?

If weekly sessions aren't quite enough, our Intensive Outpatient Program adds more time, structure, and support. We also offer family and couples therapy and group treatment.
